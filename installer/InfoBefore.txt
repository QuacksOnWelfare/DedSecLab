Before you start

DedSecLab is a defensive security lab. Use it only on systems, networks and accounts that you own or have explicit written permission to test.

You need a DedSec Lab account to sign in. Create one from the app's sign-in screen or through the community if you do not have access yet.

DedSecLab connects to your own private VPN lab servers. Make sure your VPN connection is set up before you begin.

By continuing you agree to use the software only for lawful purposes and only against targets you are authorised to test.
